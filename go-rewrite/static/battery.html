This dashboard shows anonymous battery health metrics for the fleet. No personal data is stored or displayed.

How to read the charts

One chart shows one battery size. Each car is compared with its own first measurements (100 %). The x-axis starts at the first data of each car.

Line: median of all cars. Band: the middle 50 % of the cars. A step needs data from 2 or more cars.

Left axis: capacity in kWh for the median start capacity of the group. Right axis: % of start capacity.

Note: Battery capacity is estimated from charging sessions where a significant charge was added (>20% SOC change). Cars with fewer than 3 measurements are not in the charts.
